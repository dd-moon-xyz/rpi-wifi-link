# Mobile app

- Flutter and Dart: one Android/iOS codebase, intended for store publication.
- `flutter_reactive_ble`: discover the Pi and exchange provisioning messages over BLE.
- UI: choose a Pi, authenticate, select Wi-Fi, enter credentials, and view connection status.
- Configure platform-specific Bluetooth permissions and test on real Android and iOS devices.
